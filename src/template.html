The Expanding Human Toolkit

Four long-run measures of humanity's collective capability, on one shared time axis: the number of distinct substances & materials we know, the sheer tonnage of material we put through the economy each year, the research papers we publish each year, and a composite Global Interconnectedness Index. Solid lines rest on annual source data; dashed lines are model reconstructions between literature anchor points (dots). Shaded bands are uncertainty / definitional ranges.
