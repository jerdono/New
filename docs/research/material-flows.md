## (b) Elhacham et al. 2020 (Nature 588) — anthropogenic mass

Hard anchors: ~35 Gt (1900, ≈3% of living biomass) → ~1,100 Gt (2020, crossover with living
biomass ±6 yr); current production ~30 Gt/yr; effective doubling ~20–24 yr; ~50% concrete.
Interpolated [soft]: 1920 ~62, 1940 ~110, 1960 ~195, 1980 ~345, 2000 ~615 Gt (real curve
bends up faster after 1950).

## (d) Recommended construction method

- **1900–2024 observed seam-spliced series:** Krausmann et al. 2009 (1900–2005) →
  Krausmann et al. 2018 (→2015) → UNEP IRP Global Material Flows Database /
  Schandl et al. 2024 (→2024), reconciling overlap years 1970–2015.
- **Pre-1900 anchored ranges (not a continuous account):** population × sociometabolic
  per-capita rates (Fischer-Kowalski/Haberl/Krausmann regimes: hunter-gatherer
  ~0.5–1 t/cap/yr; agrarian ~3–6; industrial ~15–25) — giving 1500 ≈ 1.4–2.9 Gt/yr,
  1700 ≈ 1.8–3.6, 1800 ≈ 3–7, 1850 ≈ 4–9. Cross-checked against coal (OWID/Smil) and
  pig-iron/steel statistics (world pig iron ~14 Mt 1875 → ~80 Mt 1913) and the HANPP
  biomass series (Krausmann et al. 2013 PNAS: 6.9 → 14.8 PgC/yr, 1910–2005).
- Present pre-1900 values as ranges; the agrarian→industrial regime transition dominates
  uncertainty, not population.
